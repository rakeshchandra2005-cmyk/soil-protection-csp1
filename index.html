01 / THE CHALLENGE

Soil is more than dirt.

It supports plant growth, stores water and carbon, and provides a home for countless organisms. But human activities can damage this vital resource.

🌧️

Soil erosion

Wind and water carry away fertile topsoil, especially when land has little plant cover.

🧪

Pollution

Improper waste disposal and harmful chemicals can contaminate soil and affect living organisms.

🌾

Loss of fertility

Repeated intensive cultivation and poor soil management can reduce nutrients and soil structure.

🏗️

Land degradation

Deforestation, construction, and overgrazing can leave land less productive and more vulnerable.

02 / TAKE ACTION

Small steps. Lasting impact.

Soil protection starts with choices at home, in our communities, and on farms.

01

Grow more plants

Trees, grasses, and ground cover help hold soil in place and reduce erosion.

🌳
02

Compost organic waste

Composting suitable food and garden waste can return organic matter to soil.

♻️
03

Use chemicals responsibly

Follow recommended application guidance and avoid dumping oils, paints, or chemicals on land.

🧤
04

Keep land covered

Mulch, cover crops, and careful water management help protect the soil surface.
